# Cover Letter — IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please consider the manuscript “Drift-Triggered Conformal Quality Gating for Industrial IoT under Production Shift” for publication as a Regular Research Paper in IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics.

The manuscript addresses an industrial-informatics problem that arises when an AI quality model is used during production change while inspection labels arrive with delay. It presents a drift-triggered conformal quality gate that couples an unlabeled batch drift score, class-conditional conformal calibration, a conservative first-shift inspection hold, delayed-label rolling recalibration, and a batch-level audit event. The contribution is an operational decision layer for industrial IoT quality inspection, rather than a claim of a new conformal-prediction theorem.

The topic fits the journal through its direct connection to industrial IoT, quality inspection, human-in-the-loop escalation, and digital-thread traceability. The experiments use two public manufacturing proxies, UCI SECOM and AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance, under chronological, leakage-controlled evaluation. On SECOM, probability-threshold and pooled conformal baselines miss 81.3% and 79.5% of failures, while class-conditional conformal gating and the proposed policy miss 3.3% and inspect 80.8% and 82.0% of units, respectively. On AI4I, the proposed policy misses 10.1% of failures and inspects 11.1% of units. The manuscript explicitly states that these are public-proxy results and does not claim Airbus, aircraft-production, or post-shift distribution-free validation.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration by another journal. The final author list is Siqiang Zhao (corresponding author) and Fengxiang Zhang. The authors declare no competing interests and no external funding for this study. Data and code availability, CRediT authorship, and generative-AI disclosure are stated in the manuscript.
